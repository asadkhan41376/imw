Quick Commerce

Zepto Seller Services

Zepto is one of India’s fastest-growing quick commerce platforms, delivering groceries, essentials, and FMCG products within minutes, making it a powerful channel for hyperlocal sales and instant business growth. Selling on Zepto allows brands to reach high-intent customers and scale rapidly with demand-driven purchases. Our Zepto seller services include complete support from registration to onboarding, where we assist with eligibility requirements such as GST, FSSAI, PAN, bank details, and product catalog submission, followed by smooth account approval and dashboard access.

We further optimize product listings with SEO-focused content and pricing strategies, manage inventory and order fulfillment for seamless operations, and run targeted PPC campaigns to boost visibility and conversions. Additionally, we focus on performance optimization to increase revenue, handle customer support and reviews to maintain brand reputation, and ensure compliance with platform policies while providing reinstatement support for uninterrupted business operations.

BigBasket Seller Services

BigBasket is India’s leading online grocery marketplace, connecting sellers with millions of customers seeking fresh produce, FMCG products, and daily essentials. It offers a strong opportunity for businesses to establish a reliable online presence and achieve consistent sales growth. Our BigBasket seller services cover the complete journey from registration and documentation to onboarding, ensuring compliance with GST, FSSAI, and KYC requirements while setting up bank details and product listings efficiently.

We enhance product visibility through SEO-optimized listings and proper categorization, manage inventory and order fulfillment for smooth operations, and implement keyword-driven ranking strategies to improve discoverability. Our advertising solutions include sponsored campaigns designed for higher conversions, while our performance optimization strategies focus on pricing and revenue growth. We also handle customer support and reputation management, ensuring positive reviews and trust, along with continuous compliance monitoring and account protection to maintain long-term success on the platform.

Swiggy Instamart Seller Services

Swiggy Instamart is a rapidly expanding quick commerce platform that enables instant delivery of groceries and essentials, helping businesses tap into a high-demand customer base. Selling on Instamart provides access to a fast-moving market where speed and visibility directly impact sales. Our services include complete seller registration support, ensuring all eligibility requirements such as GST, FSSAI, PAN, bank details, and product catalog submission are fulfilled, followed by smooth onboarding and dashboard activation. We optimize product listings with SEO-friendly titles, images, and keywords to improve search visibility, while managing inventory and order processes for efficient fulfillment.

Our team also implements advanced SEO and ranking strategies, runs targeted ads and promotional campaigns, and focuses on performance optimization to drive consistent revenue growth. In addition, we manage customer interactions, reviews, and complaints to maintain brand credibility, while ensuring compliance with platform policies and providing support for suspension prevention and account recovery when needed.

Blinkit Seller Services

Blinkit (formerly Grofers) is a leading quick commerce platform offering ultra-fast delivery of groceries, FMCG products, and daily essentials, enabling sellers to reach a large customer base seeking instant convenience. Selling on Blinkit helps brands achieve rapid visibility and consistent order flow in a highly competitive market. Our Blinkit seller services include end-to-end support starting from account registration, where we assist with GST, FSSAI, PAN, bank details, and product catalog submission, followed by smooth onboarding and seller dashboard activation.

We enhance product listings with SEO-driven content and proper categorization to improve discoverability, while managing inventory and order fulfillment for seamless operations. Our team also focuses on keyword-based ranking strategies, runs sponsored ad campaigns for increased reach, and implements performance optimization techniques to boost revenue. Additionally, we handle customer support and reputation management to maintain trust, along with compliance monitoring and account protection to ensure long-term stability and growth on the platform.
